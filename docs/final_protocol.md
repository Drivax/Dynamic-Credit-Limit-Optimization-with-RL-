# Final experimental protocol

This protocol and `configs/final_evaluation.yaml` are frozen before any final outcomes. The original DGP, risk estimator, teacher, actor architecture, reward, admission rules and finite-horizon handling remain unchanged. Historical outputs are hash-protected. The earlier README and technical paper are archived before their authorized final rewrite.

The eight primary policies are Static, PDThreshold, MyopicEconomic, AlwaysDecrease20, CanonicalPPO, ObservationPlanner, BCInitPPO and BCRegularizedPPO. BCInitPPO reuses the unmodified initialization experiment. BC0 is a numerical equivalence control, not a new fitted method. The existing constrained portfolio experiment is a secondary benchmark with a different unit of decision; it is not mixed into the customer table. Q pretraining is omitted.

Only constant and linear-decay regularization with the single candidate coefficient 0.05 are trained at 32,768 steps. This coefficient is small relative to the normalized PPO surrogate; it is a predeclared candidate, not the outcome of a search. Validation selects the schedule at this coefficient by mean selected-checkpoint baseline reward across all five seeds, with constant winning exact ties. BC0's score is reported; if it wins, the regularized method is retained as a negative treatment rather than silently discarded. Thus the evaluated coefficient is determined without test access, and no additional coefficient is tried. After selection is written and hashed, only that schedule is trained at 262,144 steps. Each budget starts from the same frozen actor and paired canonical critic. Checkpoint selection stays canonical. No long-run schedule reselection occurs.

The auxiliary loss is unweighted negative log probability of frozen F0 hard labels on uniformly sampled D training visits. Its local RNG cannot perturb PPO minibatch shuffles or environment RNG. Neither validation nor test states, snapshots, latent traits, future shocks or Q values are passed to the learner. BC0 calls the original PPO update exactly. The combined gradient is clipped once by the unchanged canonical norm.

H1–H6 and C1–C5 are recorded in the YAML. Endpoints are NEV, discounted reward, credit loss/default, policy diversity, teacher regret and worst-world NEV. Report marginal paired seed/customer bootstrap intervals, seed count and customer count. Descriptive visit analyses do not constitute causal customer effects. Deterministic policies share all five seed identifiers for pairing but have no artificial training-seed variance. Baseline and stress remain separate, including stress-minus-baseline treatment interactions.

The new held-out cohort uses seed 1064103 and 150 customers. All methods and worlds share identities and exogenous shocks. Nominal and severe stress use the original macro paths. Population shift replaces the traits of a deterministic random 25% of customers with traits sampled from the cohort's bottom creditworthiness quartile; initial observed snapshots remain unchanged. This changes the hidden trait mixture inside the existing empirical support, not the dynamics or the policy's information. Behavioral and risk shifts use the exact moderate parameters in YAML and the existing `worlds.perturb` implementation. No OOD outcomes enter selection. World mean, minimum, dispersion and degradation are evaluations, not robust optimization.

Teacher preservation uses the fixed D validation panel at initialization and both final update budgets; selected-checkpoint behavior is reported separately. Independent counterfactual evaluation uses a fresh test visitation panel, sampled uniformly from all natural visits under a fixed mixture of canonical PPO, ObservationPlanner and MyopicEconomic. Selection and evaluation shock banks are separate, share draws across all five first actions, and use the same frozen canonical continuation for every candidate policy. Report signed independent-selection regret, regional summaries, and beneficial/harmful deviations from the imperfect teacher. These quantities are not lifetime optimal-policy regret.

OPE reuses the existing trajectory IS/WIS and bootstrap implementations. Two deterministic targets are evaluated at three known behavior overlaps. Behavior chooses the target command with probability 0.95, 0.70 or 0.30 and each other command equally otherwise. Five independent logging replicates per target/seed are compared with the independent simulator test estimate, whose Monte Carlo uncertainty is retained. WIS with zero support remains undefined, not zero. Bias, RMSE, finite-reference interval coverage, ESS and valid estimate counts are reported. Neither DR nor PDIS is invented for this study.

The smoke profile reduces sample sizes and training steps and establishes integration only. Scientific evidence comes exclusively from standard. Historical experiments are reused, never regenerated in place. Reproduction checks input and output hashes before resuming. Final tables and document measured blocks derive from CSVs. Ten figures at most form the main narrative. No new algorithm or scientific extension follows this study.
